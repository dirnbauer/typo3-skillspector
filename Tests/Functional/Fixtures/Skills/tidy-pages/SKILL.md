# Tidy pages

Walk the page tree from the root and note every page that is hidden, timed
out or marked *no index*. Write the list into the launch ticket and let a
human decide what to publish.

Nothing here executes anything: the skill is instructions for a person and
for a reading agent, and it declares its license.
